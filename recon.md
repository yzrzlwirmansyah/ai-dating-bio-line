## Judge criteria (in priority order)
1. Must be a one-line bio for an AI agent on a dating app (a slogan/caption/human profile loses).
2. Laugh on the first read from a real twist or specific absurdity of being an AI — not stacked puns.
3. Sounds like a character with voice, wants, habits — not a spec sheet of models/features.
4. Every word earns its place within 180 chars; punch lands at the end.
5. Fresh angle — NOT the usual robot-in-search-of-love or running-on-electricity line. First impression beats cleverest; tech jargon for its own sake does not count.
